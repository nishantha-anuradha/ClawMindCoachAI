# 🧠 ClawMind Coach AI — Smart Crypto Trading Assistant

Stop trading with emotions. Start trading with intelligence.

ClawMind Coach AI is a simple AI assistant designed to help Binance users understand the market, improve trading behavior, and make smarter decisions.

⚠️ DISCLAIMER:
- This is NOT a token or cryptocurrency
- This is NOT affiliated with Binance
- This is a personal project for the OpenClaw AI Contest

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📊 Market Insights
- Get simple explanations of market trends
- Understand bullish vs bearish movements

### 🧠 Behavior Coaching
- Detect emotional trading patterns
- Warn about FOMO and panic selling

### 💡 Smart Suggestions
- Basic trading tips
- Risk management guidance

### 📚 Beginner Friendly
- Explains crypto in simple English
- Helps new users learn step by step

---

## 💻 How It Works

User asks:
"Is BTC a good buy?"

AI responds:
"BTC is showing bullish momentum, but resistance is near. Consider risk before entering."

---

## 🎯 Purpose

Most traders lose money because of emotions.

ClawMind Coach AI helps users:
- Stay disciplined
- Avoid bad decisions
- Learn while trading
